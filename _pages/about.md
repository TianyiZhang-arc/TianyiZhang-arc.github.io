I am a first-year PhD student in the [Mobile Robotics Lab at ETH Zurich](https://mrl.ethz.ch/), supervised by [Prof. Stefan Leutenegger](https://mavt.ethz.ch/de/personen/person-detail.MTEyOTE0.TGlzdC8xMzc1LC0xNzA2OTc4MDE3.html) since June 2025. 
My current research primarily focuses on **3D reconstruction** and **visual SLAM**. 

I received my Master’s degree in [Robotics, Systems and Control](https://master-robotics.ethz.ch/) from ETH Zurich, where I worked on Structure-from-Motion research projects under the supervision of [Prof. Marc Pollefeys](https://cvg.ethz.ch/team/Prof-Dr-Marc-Pollefeys) and [Prof. Federico Tombari](https://federicotombari.github.io/). Prior to that, I earned my Bachelor’s degree from the [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://en.hit.edu.cn/). I also spent a year as a research assistant at [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/), working with [Prof. Max Q.-H. Meng](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/faculties/mengqinghu.html).
